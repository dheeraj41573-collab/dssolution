## Graphic design

### What graphic design services do you offer?

Our creative services include logo and brand identity design, social media graphics, banners, advertisements, presentations, website graphics and other marketing materials.

### How can design help build my brand?

A consistent logo, colour palette, typography and visual style help customers recognise your business across your website, social channels and marketing.

### Can you redesign an existing logo?

Yes. We can review your existing identity and discuss a refresh that keeps the elements important to your brand while updating its visual direction.

### How much do design services cost?

Pricing depends on the type and scope of work, number of deliverables, revisions and required formats. We confirm the scope and quotation before work begins.

### How long does a design project take?

Timelines depend on the project and feedback cycle. We agree on milestones and expected timing before starting.

## Websites and applications

### Which website platforms and technologies do you work with?

Depending on the requirements, we work with platforms such as WordPress, Shopify and Wix, as well as custom technologies including PHP, Laravel, Node.js, React, Next.js and NestJS.

### Can you improve my existing website?

Yes. We can discuss design, usability, content, performance or development improvements based on your website and priorities.

## Marketing

### Do you guarantee search rankings or advertising results?

No. Search and advertising outcomes depend on factors such as competition, budget, audience, platform changes and the work completed. We set clear goals, report on performance and use results to guide improvements.

### How do you decide which marketing service is right for my business?

We start with your business goals, customers, current digital presence and budget, then discuss which channels and services may be a good fit.

### How can I get a proposal?

[Contact our team](/contact) with a short description of your goals and we will follow up to discuss the next steps.
